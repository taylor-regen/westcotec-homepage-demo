About us

Built in Norfolk. Proven on roads across Europe and beyond.

Since 2001, Westcotec have designed and manufactured in their Norfolk factory — high-quality, rigorously tested materials and components, with manufacture, technical support, R&D, sales and marketing across two joined sites.

That flexibility has led to custom work not only in the UK but across Northern Europe, Asia and the Middle East. In 2018, Westcotec became a fully-fledged employee-owned company — one of only a few road safety equipment manufacturers with CE, UKCA and UKNI marks across their range.
